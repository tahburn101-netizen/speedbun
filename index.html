About Speed Bun
Birmingham's Premier Used Car Specialist

For over a decade, Speed Bun has been Birmingham's most trusted name in quality used cars. Born and built right here in the city, we've spent 10+ years getting to know our customers, understanding what they need, and delivering vehicles that genuinely exceed expectations. We're not a faceless forecourt — we're a local business that takes real pride in every single sale. Every car in our showroom is personally hand-picked, thoroughly inspected, and prepared to the highest standard before it ever reaches you. Whether you're searching for a refined executive saloon, a practical family hatch, or a head-turning sports car, our team has the knowledge and the stock to find your perfect match. No pushy tactics, no hidden surprises — just honest advice, fair prices, and a buying experience you'll actually enjoy. That's the Speed Bun promise, and it's one we've been keeping for Birmingham drivers for over ten years.
